Eligibility

Annual income of $100,000 or more is required to be eligible for this offer. This offer is for residents of, and participants from, the United States, including the District of Columbia, but excluding Delaware and Maine. Persons who are also not eligible for this offer include: (i) those who have attended a sales presentation with the last 6 months with the MVCI corporate direct sales office; (ii) those who have an MVCI contract pending or on hold; (iii) Owners who have delinquent maintenance fees or are in foreclosure; and (iv) MVCI associates.

Offer

All eligible parties must complete a 90-minute virtual program overview and sales presentation via telephone or video platform in order to receive Marriott Bonvoy points with internet access with an authorized MVCI Sales Professional. Participants cannot be represented by a third party. If married, engaged or living together as a couple, both must participate together sharing the same tablet or computer for the virtual presentation. Upon booking of the virtual program, participants will receive a link with instructions to join the program overview and both must participate in the program overview. Both must be active participants in the program overview and virtual sales presentation for 90 minutes. Eligible parties requesting sales presentation premier time slots will receive 15,000 Marriott Bonvoy points, and those requesting other sales presentation premier time slots will receive 20,000 Marriott Bonvoy Points. Those participating as an individual and who are requesting sales presentation premier time slots will receive 7,500 Marriott Bonvoy points, and those requesting other sales presentation time slots will receive 10,000 Marriott Bonvoy points. Every effort will be made to accommodate your preferred time. Those joining who do not activate the video and audio recording button for the majority of the meeting will not be ineligible for this offer. The Marriott Bonvoy points will be deposited into the one account provided by the participant approximately 6 to 8 weeks after completion.
