Fun-Filled Attractions for the Family

Experience the best of Orlando with a hotel-style timeshare close to world-famous attractions or a tranquil resort retreat that reveals the city’s sophisticated side. Create magical family memories at our Orlando timeshare resorts.
  • Feel the nonstop thrills of Walt Disney World® and Universal Orlando®.
  • Savor culinary delights in the Mills 50 neighborhood, from endless tacos to New American fine dining.
  • Relax on the white sands of Daytona and New Smyrna beaches under the warm Florida sun.
  • Glide over crystal-clear waters on a glass-bottom boat tour at Silver Springs State Park.
  • Watch playful manatees at Blue Spring State Park or explore wildlife at a nearby safari.
  • Discover a grown-up's playground with museums, boutiques, wine bars, and craft breweries in historic Winter Park.
  • Immerse yourself in arts and culture with museums and theaters in Ivanhoe Village.
